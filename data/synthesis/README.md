# Demo data

All companies, people, parts, and prices in the synthetic files are fictional. Running [make_spares.py](make_spares.py) and [make_handbook.py](make_handbook.py) reproduces the generated documents.

## Public source

`data/mlc/mlc_2006_consolidated_2022.pdf` is the International Labour Office's consolidated text of the Maritime
Labour Convention, 2006, including the 2022 amendments, downloaded unchanged on 25 November 2026 from
<https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-10/NORMES_MLC%20Amendments-EN_2022_Web_1.pdf>.
It is the ILO's publication and is not covered by this repository's authorship.
