# Staff Leave Policy (synthetic, Harbourview Services)

## 1. Annual leave

Full-time staff receive 24 days of paid annual leave in each calendar year.
Part-time staff receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.
Annual leave requests must be submitted to the line manager at least 14 days before the first day of leave.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days expire on 31 March.

## 2. Sick leave

Staff receive up to 10 days of paid sick leave in each calendar year.
A staff member who is sick must tell the line manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence.
A medical certificate is required for any sickness absence longer than 3 consecutive working days.
Unused sick leave is not carried over and is not paid out.

## 3. Parental leave

A parent who gives birth receives 26 weeks of paid maternity leave after 12 months of continuous service.
A non-birthing parent receives 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 8 weeks of the birth.
Adoptive parents receive the same leave as birth parents, counted from the date of placement.

## 4. Compassionate leave

Staff receive 5 days of paid compassionate leave after the death of a partner, child or parent.
Staff receive 2 days of paid compassionate leave after the death of a grandparent or sibling.

## 5. Study leave

Study leave of up to 4 days per year may be granted for an approved course.
The training committee decides study leave requests at its monthly meeting.
Staff who leave the company within 12 months of study leave must repay the course fee.

## 6. Public holidays

Staff who work on a public holiday receive a day off in lieu, to be taken within 60 days.
